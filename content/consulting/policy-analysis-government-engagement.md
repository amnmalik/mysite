Research that stays in journals does not change policy. A central part of my work has always been translating modelling-based findings into actionable recommendations for policymakers — and then engaging directly with those policymakers to communicate findings clearly and credibly.

## What I do

- **Policy briefs and reports** — writing evidence-based policy documents that translate complex modelling outputs into clear, actionable recommendations for non-technical audiences
- **Government engagement** — presenting research findings directly to ministries, regulatory bodies, parliamentary bodies, and multilateral institutions
- **Strategic analysis** — assessing the feasibility, implications, and trade-offs of specific policy options in India's energy transition and climate commitments
- **Stakeholder facilitation** — moderating and participating in policy dialogues, expert panels, and multi-stakeholder consultations

## Past work

- Presented findings on India's ETS and net-zero targets at **Niti Aayog**
- Delivered a lecture on climate change at the **Rajya Sabha** (Upper House of Parliament) to interns of Members of Parliament
- Spoke at the **Indian Energy Forum**, the **Carbon Markets Association of India**, and multiple state-level dialogues in Gujarat and Uttar Pradesh
- Engaged with **UNDP**, **ICRIER**, and the **School of Public Policy** on just transition and MSME decarbonisation
- Worked directly with state government departments on net-zero strategies: with GUVNL and GETRI in Gujarat and the Planning Department in Uttarakhand.

## Who I work with

Government ministries, state governments, parliamentary bodies, multilateral organisations, think tanks, and civil society organisations working on energy and climate policy.
