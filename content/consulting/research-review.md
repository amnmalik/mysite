Rigorous peer review is the backbone of credible science. I contribute to this process as a reviewer for several leading international journals, bringing expertise in integrated assessment modelling, Indian energy policy, and climate economics.

## Journals I review for

- **Nature**
- **Environmental Research Letters**
- **Environmental Research Letters: Climate**
- **Climate Policy**
- **Energy Policy**
- **Energy Research and Social Science**
- **Climate Economics**

In 2023, I was recognised by IOP Publishing as an **IOP Trusted Reviewer** for exceptionally high level of peer review consistency.

## What I offer

Beyond journal peer review, I also provide independent review of:

- **Research reports and policy briefs** — pre-publication review for think tanks, research organisations, and government agencies. I've reviewed for several publications within CEEW as well as other think tanks such as ICRIER.
- **Modelling studies** — technical review of energy system and integrated assessment modelling work, including methodology, assumptions, and analysis.


## Who I work with

Academic publishers, think tanks, research organisations, government agencies, and foundations commissioning independent research.
