Building the next generation of energy and climate analysts requires more than textbooks. I bring real-world modelling experience and policy engagement into training settings — making complex concepts accessible without losing rigour.

## What I do

- **Guest lectures** — delivering lectures on energy economics, climate modelling, integrated assessment, and decarbonisation policy at universities and professional programmes
- **Executive training** — workshops for senior management at energy companies, financial institutions, and government agencies on understanding the energy transition and its implications
- **Capacity building programmes** — multi-session training designed to build analytical capability within organisations and institutions
- **Workshop facilitation** — designing and facilitating participatory workshops that combine technical content with policy dialogue
