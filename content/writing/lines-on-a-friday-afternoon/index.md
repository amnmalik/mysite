---
title: "Lines on a Friday Afternoon"
date: 2015-02-20
summary: "Two short poems from February 2015."
tags: ["poetry"]
---

I've been run down
I've been run down
Trampled but breathing
Bruised but alive
These feet look ugly from below
Deformed sticks, that's what they are
Suffocating others, their presence is unwanted
How does it feel to walk on mishap bodies?
You have good shoes, I believe.

I can barely see the light from below
I can barely think from below
Only when I trample others can I walk
I will suffer this, then
Be dead than alive
Till I am completely numb
And my mind is my universe.

---

How am I different?
It seems
my thoughts have been thought,
my ideas have been invented,
my feelings felt.
I have a unique face, but then I am not sure.

I'm not a genius, you know, just a farm boy —
a farm boy wanting to be different,
wanting a life that's not found,
singing a song not sung,
writing a poem not written.
I'll be back when I can think of something better.
I hope I'm not completely lost.

I still sleep, eat food, drink beer.
There are still forces greater than my mind,
ticking across the soul of the world.
You could die without them.
Sometimes you do.
