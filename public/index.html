Hi, I'm

Aman Malik.

I'm an independent energy researcher and policy analyst, specialising in India's energy transition and decarbonisation policy.

I work with governments, research organisations, and financial institutions on energy modelling, climate policy analysis, and climate-related financial risk. I bring a decade of experience in quantitative energy system modelling and direct engagement with policymakers across India and Europe.

About Me

PhD from the Technical University of Berlin and the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research. Until recently, Programme Lead at the Low Carbon Economy team at CEEW in New Delhi; previously researcher and postdoctoral fellow at PIK in Germany.

I take on consulting engagements in energy modelling, climate policy analysis, and transition risk.

Consulting

I work with governments, financial institutions, research organisations, and think-tanks on questions at the intersection of energy systems, climate policy, and economics. Engagements typically involve one or more of the following:
Energy System Modelling
Scenario development and modelling using tools such as GCAM, REMIND, and state-level energy models. Applications include net-zero pathways, power sector planning, and policy impact assessment.
Learn more →
Carbon Markets & ETS
Policy design and analysis of carbon pricing mechanisms like emission trading schemes, and carbon credit frameworks - with particular expertise in the Indian context.
Learn more →
Transition Risk & Climate Finance
Country-specific scenario development to inform climate-related financial risk assessment for banks, regulators, and institutional investors.
Learn more →
Policy Analysis & Government Engagement
Evidence-based policy briefs, research reports on India's energy transition and decarbonisation - delivered directly to government ministries, regulatory bodies, and multilateral institutions including Niti Aayog, the Reserve Bank of India, and the Ministry of Power.
Learn more →
Training & Capacity Building
Guest lectures, workshops, and executive training on energy economics, climate modelling, and decarbonisation policy for government officials, financial institutions, and academic audiences.
Learn more →
Research Review
Peer review of research papers and reports for leading international journals including Nature, Environmental Research Letters, Climate Policy, Energy Policy, Energy Research and Social Science, and Climate Economics.
